# HOME.md

## Purpose

The Home page is the main landing page for Sharon Fellowship Church of Albany.

It should give visitors a strong first impression and quickly answer:

- What church is this?
- Where is it located?
- When are services?
- How can I watch online?
- How can I contact or visit?
- What does this church value?

The page should feel modern, welcoming, clean, faith-centered, and visually polished.

---

## Main Goal

The Home page should guide visitors through a simple journey:

1. Welcome them
2. Show service times
3. Communicate SFCA’s identity
4. Introduce leadership
5. Show church life
6. Point to livestream
7. Invite them to visit or contact the church

Do not make the page feel cluttered.

The homepage should be visually strong, but still easy to use.

---

## Target Audience

The Home page should serve:

- Christians looking for a church near Albany, NY
- Families looking for a church community
- People looking for Malayalam, Telugu, or English services
- People in the Albany / Capital Region area
- New visitors
- Current members looking for quick links
- Online viewers
- Students or young adults looking for fellowship

The tone should be warm, sincere, and inviting.

---

## Design Direction

Follow `DESIGN.md`.

The Home page should feel:

- Blue-centered
- Light and modern
- Clean and spacious
- Smoothly animated
- Premium but welcoming
- Faith-centered
- Not generic
- Not overly dark
- Not cluttered

Use the current SFCA homepage only as a content reference.

Do not copy the old layout.

If more content is needed, refer to:

`https://www.sfcalbany.com/`
