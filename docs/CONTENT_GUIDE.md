# CONTENT_GUIDE.md

## Purpose

This file defines the content rules for the Sharon Fellowship Church of Albany website.

The goal is to keep the website content accurate, respectful, clear, faith-centered, and welcoming.

Codex should use this file when writing, editing, organizing, or placing content across the website.

Do not invent church facts, pastor information, branch church details, service times, events, ministries, testimonies, or doctrinal statements.

---

## Main Content Goal

The website should communicate who Sharon Fellowship Church of Albany is in a clear and welcoming way.

The content should help visitors quickly understand:

- Who the church is
- Where the church is located
- When services happen
- How to watch live
- How to contact the church
- What the church believes
- Who the pastors/leaders are
- What missions and branch churches are connected
- How to get involved or visit

The content should feel warm, sincere, and church-appropriate.

It should not feel fake, overly corporate, overly casual, or generic.

Content Accuracy Rules

Accuracy is more important than filling every empty space.

Rules:

Do not invent new church facts.
Do not invent branch church details.
Do not invent pastor biographies.
Do not invent service times.
Do not invent ministry names.
Do not invent events.
Do not invent giving links.
Do not invent testimonies.
Do not invent doctrinal statements.
Do not use fake statistics.
Do not use fake pastor photos.
Do not use fake quotes from leaders.

If information is missing, use a clean placeholder or TODO comment.

Example:

TODO: Add confirmed branch church information.
Content Source Priority

Use content in this order of priority:

Confirmed information from this project’s markdown files
Confirmed information provided directly by the user
Existing content from the current SFCA website
Clearly marked placeholders or TODOs

Do not make assumptions when important church information is missing.

Church Name Usage

Use the full church name for formal sections:

Sharon Fellowship Church of Albany

Use the short name only when appropriate:

SFCA

Good usage:

Welcome to Sharon Fellowship Church of Albany.
At SFCA, we gather for worship, prayer, and fellowship.

Do not overuse the abbreviation before the full name is introduced.

Location Usage

The website should clearly mention Albany, NY where appropriate.

Important location references:

Albany, NY
1781 Western Ave, Albany, NY 12203
Capital Region
Upstate New York

Use location naturally for SEO and visitor clarity.

Good:

Join us this Sunday at Sharon Fellowship Church of Albany, located at 1781 Western Ave in Albany, NY.

Bad:

Albany church Albany NY church in Albany NY near Albany NY.

Scripture and Doctrine Rules

Be very careful with doctrine and belief content.

Rules:

Do not rewrite doctrine in a way that changes meaning.
Do not simplify theological statements so much that meaning is lost.
Do not add doctrinal claims that are not already provided.
Do not paraphrase belief statements too aggressively.
Preserve scripture references.
Use clean formatting to improve readability.
If the current website has belief sections, organize them clearly without changing the message.

Good improvements:

Breaking long belief text into sections
Adding headings
Using accordions
Fixing spacing
Fixing obvious typos
Improving readability

Bad improvements:

Changing theological wording
Adding new beliefs
Removing important doctrine
Making doctrine vague
Missions Content Rules

The Missions page should communicate the church’s heart for outreach and service.

Rules:

Use current website content where available.
Do not invent specific mission trips or organizations.
Do not invent donation links.
Do not invent outreach numbers.
Keep the tone service-focused and Christ-centered.
Use placeholders for missing confirmed details.

Good tone:

Our mission is to serve others with the love of Christ and share the hope of the Gospel.

Avoid:

We are transforming the world through our unmatched global network.

Branch church information must be accurate.

Rules:

Do not invent branch church addresses.
Do not invent branch pastors.
Do not invent branch church social links.

Gallery content should feel real and church-centered.

Youth

Live Page Content Rules

The Live page should help visitors watch online easily.

Official links:

Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/sfcalbany

Rules:
